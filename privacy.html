Privacy Policy: Meadowlark's World: Syllable Quest

Effective: November 7, 2026

Meadowlark's World: Syllable Quest is a reading game for children. We built it so that it collects nothing about your child.

The short version

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The game never asks for names or email addresses.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies.
  • Nothing your child does in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser's own storage on this device so your child can pick up where they left off:

  • progress for each reader on this device (at most two): the placement check, which sounds and words have been practiced and how they went, the date, length and accuracy of each session, and the crests, passes and Word Book pages earned
  • settings, such as the current stage, session length, sound on or off, voice speed and display choices, and the color picked for the reader's character
  • the grown-up PIN, saved only in scrambled (hashed) form, never as the digits themselves
  • voice recordings, only if a grown-up makes them in the grown-up corner
  • a safety copy of the saved progress, kept on this device when a game update changes how progress is saved
  • a copy of the game's own files, so it can run without an internet connection

Anything your child writes on the on-screen writing pad is never saved.

None of this is sent to us, and we cannot see it. It is not backed up online. A grown-up can choose to save a backup file (Backup, in the grown-up corner) or download a tutor report or tricky-pairs list; those files are saved only where you choose to put them.

The microphone

The game asks for the microphone only when a grown-up presses Record on the Recordings screen in the grown-up corner. Recordings are saved on this device and played back only on this device (and are included in a backup file if you save one). Your browser will ask your permission first, and you can say no; the game works without it.

The browser's built-in voice

When the game has no recording for a line, it may ask your browser's built-in voice to read the game's own text aloud. You can turn this off in Settings. Some browsers use an online speech service for their voices; only the game's words are read this way, never anything about your child.

Hosting

The game's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect its service. We do not receive or use those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

The game is made for children, including children under 13. Because we do not collect, use or share personal information from children, we do not need to ask for parental consent. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for consent before collecting anything.

Deleting your data

Inside the game, in the grown-up corner:

  • Start over erases a reader's progress. The PIN, settings and voice recordings stay.
  • Remove student erases one reader's progress, settings and recordings, when two readers share the device.
  • Delete recording, on the Recordings screen, erases one recording.

To erase everything the game saved, clear this site's data in your browser settings (often under "Site settings" or "Cookies and site data"). Uninstalling the app or removing the home-screen icon may not clear it on every device.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. We will never start collecting personal information without updating this page first.
